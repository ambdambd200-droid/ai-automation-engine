## Quality filters (auto-reject)

- ❌ Hourly rate < $8/hr
- ❌ "Unpaid trial" or "test project for free"
- ❌ Vague scope ("build me an AI system")
- ❌ No company website / anonymous client
- ❌ Asks for bank info before contract
- ❌ Asks for external contact (WhatsApp, Telegram) in first message
- ❌ "Will pay after I see the work"

**Plus Arabic-platform specific:**
- ❌ Nafezly projects < 10$ (under platform minimum)
- ❌ No Arabic language in client's communication (some English clients are fine)
- ❌ Client with 0 prior projects AND suspicious description

## Channel pivot decision

**Paused (not cancelled):**
- Direct-email applications (6 sent, awaiting follow-up Jun 8)
- n8n Community forum (3 drafts ready)

**Active (new primary):**
- Mostaql — largest Arabic freelance platform
- Nafezly — simpler, beginner-friendly

**Why Arabic platforms:**
1. Geographic fit (user is in Gaza, Arab market is primary)
2. No fake credentials needed — Arabic clients care about portfolio
3. No age minimum strict (16+ on mostaql)
4. Two platforms = doubled opportunity
5. Public bidding = clearer feedback than email
6. Escrow system = safer than direct wire
